{{< rainbow >}}Ace Combat, but you're delivering pizzas.{{< /rainbow >}} A flying car, a neon city, and a timer.

{{< game src="/games/ace-crust/" title="Ace Crust" >}}

Grab pizzas at the orange beam and fly them through the green ring before they go cold. Three cold pizzas and you're fired. Everything else in the city is a target: kills pay cash, and every kill makes your car faster.

**Controls**

- `WASD` / arrows: climb, turn, dive
- `Shift`: boost
- `Space`: hold to drift, release for a turbo. Let go right as the drift meter flashes white for a perfect.
- `Q` / `E` or double-tap a turn: barrel roll. Roll through incoming fire for a perfect dodge.
- `J` / `Z`: guns
- `K` / `X`: missiles (they lock on automatically)
- `P` / `Esc`: pause
