In the metal ant hill at the center of the concrete jungle–
I hope I don’t die in this rattling, clankering {{< rainbow >}}piss/shit{{< /rainbow >}} filled metal coffin.
At the bottom, the doors open to the great ocean of humanity.
It washes over me like waves. A strong mesmerizing current.
It is {{< rainbow >}}love{{< /rainbow >}}.
It is the great connection.
The encompassing {{< rainbow >}}spirit{{< /rainbow >}}.

I step into the wave, weaving in past an old woman, and a burly construction worker. Dodging a finance slop bowl enjoyer, and a small asian lady walking faster than everyone else.
I think this would be a great place to experience the end of the world.
So deeply connected. The heart of the world.
But it would probably get real bad here real quick, if it all fell apart…
But it’s gotta be the most fun up until then. As everything improves rapidly. As we ride the {{< rainbow >}}exponential{{< /rainbow >}}.

I’m sipping my piping ramen; miso broth.
It’s a food hall, many different stalls.
A woman plays a show on her piano. Covers. 
She’s been playing for 5 hours, she says. Wow.
She’s begging for karaoke. For volunteers.
The woman from the couple sitting across from me volunteers twice. She likes the attention.
Her boyfriend is on instagram duty.
I zone it all out. Staring at my phone. Staring at my ramen.
The little chunk of impossible meat is nice. 
Reading about optimizing my agent loop. 

The weird thing about time is that it’s hard to tell where you are.
Maybe nothing changes at all. It’s just hard to believe.
This year has already been crazy. Do we really think it's gonna slow down? If it just keeps going like this, not even speeding up, it's basically already over.
It’s palpable. It’s like you know a tsunami is just on the horizon.

Am I living in a special time?
The end of the world?
I feel like it. But am I crazy?
Everything feels like… {{< rainbow >}}<i>vibes</i>{{< /rainbow >}}
