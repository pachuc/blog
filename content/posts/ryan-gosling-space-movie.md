The warm, invisible, embrace of trope
Its tendrils caressing you, 
Floating, happy, carefree, 
Fun.
But as the ignorance fades away
And cold realization sets in
Each wonderful moment now plainface and vapid
Its cheap horrid stench echoes in your soul
A deep disgust of the filth you had been lying with
Embarrassment of the pleasure you found in its tempting guiles
All that's left is 
Shame.
